Freezely - Delete Your Account

We're sorry to see you go. This page explains how to request deletion of your Freezely account and what data will be removed.

How to Request Account Deletion

  1. Send an email to nicolajhansen97@gmail.com from the email address associated with your Freezely account.
  2. Use the subject line: "Account Deletion Request"
  3. We will verify your identity and process your request within 7 business days.
  4. You will receive a confirmation email once your account and data have been deleted.

What Data Will Be Deleted

Data Type Action
Account credentials (email, password) Permanently deleted
Freezer inventory items Permanently deleted
Shopping list items Permanently deleted
Compartments / drawers Permanently deleted
Activity history Permanently deleted
Household membership Removed (household remains for other members)
Pending invitations Permanently deleted

Important

  • Account deletion is permanent and cannot be undone.
  • If you are the only owner of a household, the household will be deleted along with all shared data.
  • Other household members' personal accounts will not be affected.
  • Data stored locally on your device (e.g. biometric login, settings) must be removed by uninstalling the app.
